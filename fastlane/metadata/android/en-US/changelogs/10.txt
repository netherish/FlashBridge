Stability release for fastboot and ADB.

- Fixed fastboot flashing with the correct AOSP sequence: download, stream data, then flash the partition.
- Fixed stalled USB bulk writes and added pipelined transfers, so large images no longer take minutes.
- Fixed ADB authorization with the AOSP public-key format and additive checksum.
- Verified fastboot flashing and ADB connection on Redmi 9 and Vivo Y73.
